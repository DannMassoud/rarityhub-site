Terms of Use

Last updated: September 2026 · Draft for founder review

1. What RarityHub is

RarityHub is a price-tracking service and a classifieds board for collectables in the Gulf (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman). Our price index is built from publicly available completed sales. The Market lets collectors and shops list items for sale and contact each other. RarityHub is not a party to any sale, does not handle payments, and does not hold, ship, inspect or guarantee any item.

2. Accounts

You must be 18 or older to sell. You are responsible for what happens under your account. Keep your password private. One person, one account.

3. Selling

  • Genuine items only. Replicas, unauthorised copies, "1:1", "UA" and similar items are not allowed, whatever the description says.
  • Your own photos of the actual item. No stock photos, no photos taken from other listings.
  • Honest descriptions. Condition, grading company, grade and certificate number must match the item. Ungraded items are listed as ungraded.
  • Mandatory seller details. Legal name, display name, city, mobile number and email are required. Shops also provide their commercial registration number. Buyers see only your display name, city and badges.
  • When you remove a listing you tell us what happened (sold here, sold elsewhere, keeping it, listed by mistake) and, for a sale, the final price. Sold prices feed a separate "member sales" dataset and never change our published market values unless independently confirmed.
  • Listing limits grow with completed sales: new sellers 5 active listings, then 20, 50, and 100 for verified shops.
  • Listings expire when you choose (30, 60 or 90 days) or stay until removed. Expired listings ask you what happened and can be relisted.

4. Buying and meeting

Deal directly with the seller. Meet in a public place, inspect the item before paying, and never pay before you see it. RarityHub shows the real sold value of the same item next to the asking price so you can judge the offer; that value is information, not advice, and not a guarantee of authenticity.

5. Messaging and WhatsApp

You can message sellers in the app or, if they allow it, on WhatsApp. Phone numbers are shown only to signed-in users and only when the seller allows WhatsApp contact. Keep messages about the item. No spam, no harassment, no attempts to move a buyer to a scam. Either side can block the other, which ends contact both ways.

6. Reviews

After a seller marks an item "sold here" and picks the buyer from their conversations, that buyer may leave one review with a 1–5 rating and a comment. Reviews must be honest and about that transaction. Reviewer identities are not shown.

7. Reports, review and moderation

Anyone signed in can report a listing. A listing reported by three different people, or whose text triggers our replica filter, goes under review: it is hidden from buyers and the seller is notified. A moderator then approves it or removes it, normally within 24–72 hours, and the seller is notified either way. We may also remove listings and suspend sellers for breaking these terms, at our discretion.

8. Appeals

If your listing is removed or your seller account is suspended, you can appeal once from the notice you receive. Tell us why the decision is wrong and include what helps (receipts, photos of the item or slab, where you bought it). A moderator answers within 24–72 hours and may restore the listing, reinstate your account, or keep the decision.

9. Suspension

A suspended seller cannot list. Their active listings are taken down. Repeated or serious breaches (confirmed replicas, scams, harassment) lead to permanent removal.
