How the price index works

01

Track real sales

We collect completed sales — items that actually sold, with real prices and dates — from eBay and other marketplaces worldwide.

02

Filter the noise

Scam listings, dreamer prices and mismatched items are trimmed out. Recent sales count for more than old ones.

03

One honest value

Every item gets a single market value with a confidence score, backed by the number of real sales behind it.

Honesty is the product. When there isn't enough real data to value an item, RarityHub says "insufficient data" — we never invent a number. Items you find through RarityHub link straight to the marketplace listing, so you buy where the market actually is.
